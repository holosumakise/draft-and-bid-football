How it works

Roll → bid → reserve → place XI → chemistry → lock → play.

1 · Roll

Press Roll to reveal the next player. Every club sees the same card.

2 · Bid

Rotating turns and a 9-second clock. Raise, buy or pass — the leader never outbids itself. Only a unanimous pass discards.

Vbid / buyZ+1X+5Chold to pass
3 · Reserve

Keep 10M for every starting slot still empty. The tracker shows your ceiling.

4 · Place XI

Drag onto the pitch, or pick a player then a slot. Auto-fill sorts the best XI.

5 · Chemistry

Shared nation or club links starters: 2 = +1, 3–5 = +2, 6+ = +3. Best link only.

6 · Lock

Fill XI and bench, set your three plans, then Confirm. A locked club cannot bid.

7 · Match

Minute by minute, with live ratings and automatic second-half subs. 1×, 2× or Instant.

8 · Online

Create a room for a code, or Join one. The host runs the game; each club bids on its own device.

9 · Invest

Spare cash buys a training camp, medical programme or scouting network.
