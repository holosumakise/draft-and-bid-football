How it works

Roll → bid → reserve → place XI → chemistry → lock → play.

1 · Roll

Press Roll Draft to reveal one player from the pool. Everyone sees the same card.

2 · Bid

A live auction with rotating turn order and a 9-second shot clock. On your turn: raise (+1, +5 or a typed amount), or pass. Keyboard: + to type an amount (Enter to bid), Z is +1, X is +5, hold Space to pass. A leading club can only buy or pass — never outbid itself. A player is only discarded when every club passes, and that spends one of the room's shared skips; once skips run out, a pickup wheel assigns unwanted players to a random club at the minimum bid.

3 · Reserve

Keep 10M for every starting-XI slot you have not filled yet. The tracker shows your maximum bid and what is already reserved.

4 · Place XI

Drag players onto the pitch, or click a player then click a slot. Auto-fill picks the best fit; the pitch shows how each assignment is rated.

5 · Chemistry

Starters who share a nation link up: 2 linked = +1 each, 3 or more = +3 each. Linked players get a glowing ring.

6 · Lock

Fill all 11 starters and the bench, choose your in-possession, out-of-possession and transition plan, then Confirm Formation. Your plan changes chance creation, control, counters, cards, stamina and set pieces. You cannot bid while your XI is locked — unlock it when you want to spend again.

7 · Match

Matches play minute by minute with a live timeline, ratings and stats. Fatigue is tracked, so the bench is used automatically in the second half. Speed: 1×, 2× or Instant.

8 · Online

Pick a mode, then Create Room for a code or Join Room with one. The host sets squad size and difficulty and runs the game; every manager bids, picks their XI and sets tactics on their own device.
